# Script decisions

Where the source doc leaves a gap, contradicts itself, or says something the
artwork can't support — and what the live flow does about it.

Everything marked **Decided for you** is a judgement call, not an instruction.
Override any of it.

---

## 1. What the doc leaves blank

### Price
The CTA reads "Unlock My Full Sketch". No amount appears anywhere in either
version.

**What's live:** `Unlock My Full Sketch — $XX`, in both versions. Deliberately
not a real number so it can't ship by accident, and it lets the button be
judged at its true width. **Still needs a real figure.**

### What happens after they buy
The doc ends at the button. No confirmation, no thank-you, no "what next".

**What's live:** a closing stage written from scratch — she thanks them by
name, says she's picking the pencil back up, promises the inbox within 24
hours, and the conversation ends. *Decided for you.*

⚠️ It simulates a completed purchase. Nothing is charged and no email is sent.
Fine for a demo; it must not go in front of real traffic until a real checkout
sits behind the CTA, or it tells people they've bought something they haven't.

### Gender of the soulmate
Never asked, never stated. Every reference in the doc is "they/them".

**What's live:** no gender question, one artwork set, and the art is a woman —
so the flow is effectively woman-only. *Decided for you.* Adding the choice
means a second full set of five drawings.

### The trait list
Given once, at Stage 5, explicitly labelled *"Example"*: hazel eyes, olive
skin, calm deliberate voice, notices details, guarded until safe. Stage 8 says
"personality panel on the right" and gives no words at all.

**What's live:** the doc's five at Stage 5, plus three written from scratch at
Stage 8 so the list visibly grows — *"Slow to laugh — means it when they do"*,
*"Remembers what you said months ago"*, *"Steadier than they first appear"*.
*Decided for you.* **Placeholder — needs a real copy pass.**

### The personality panel
The doc asks for it *inside* the image, beside the face. The artwork doesn't
have one.

**What's live:** Selene types the traits as a chat message after posting each
sketch. Keeps the desk photographs uncluttered and the copy editable without
redrawing. *Decided for you.*

## 2. Lines in the doc that don't hold up

### "I need that third thing I mentioned."
She never mentions three things. She asks for a name, a birth date and an
email — three, but never announced as three.

**What's live:** changed to *"I need one more thing from you."* The alternative
is a line early on ("I need three things from you"), which would let the
original callback stand.

### "It's rough, I know. But this is what came through."
Written for a quick scribble. The place drawing is the most finished piece in
the set — detailed foliage, rendered water, full composition.

**What's live:** cut. Apologising for the strongest image costs more than it
earns.

### "Look at the right side. That's their profile forming next to the face."
Only works if the panel is in the image. It isn't.

**What's live:** replaced with *"Here's what I have on them so far."*

### "All 12 placements. The whole sky as it stood the moment you arrived."
A birth chart needs a time and a place. She asks for neither — only a date.

**What's live:** unchanged. Nobody will check, but it's a claim the flow
doesn't support.

### "Two came up… let me triangulate."
She then says *"I've honed it down to one"* with no account of how, or what
became of the other.

**What's live:** unchanged. A line resolving it would close the loop.

- **Three extra traits** at Stage 8 — invented so the list grows.
- **The browser tab badge** — counts Selene's messages since the visitor last
  replied, resets on every interaction.

## 4. Where A and B differ

Only two places, both exactly as the doc specifies:

- A has the place interruption; B doesn't
- A's CTA promises the meeting place; B's doesn't

Everything else is identical — stages 1–5 are literally the same file. Anything
the test shows is attributable to the place stage alone.
